Secure file storage

Files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a fresh random key. That key is locked with each recipient's RSA-3072 public key, and the owner signs every file with Ed25519. Your password unlocks your private keys. It is never stored anywhere, and without it nobody can open your files, including whoever runs this server.
